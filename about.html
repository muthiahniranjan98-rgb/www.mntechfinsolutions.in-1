WHO WE ARE

A professional bridge between customers and lending institutions.

MN TECH FINSOLUTIONS PVT LTD is focused on loan sourcing, customer assistance and application coordination. We work to understand the customer's requirement first, explain the typical documentation process and help coordinate the enquiry with suitable banks or NBFCs where applicable.

We believe financial assistance should begin with clear information—not unrealistic promises. That is why our approach focuses on professional communication, responsible follow-up and transparency about the role of the lender in the final decision.

Every bank and NBFC has its own eligibility, credit, documentation and underwriting policies. Final approval, loan amount, interest rate, tenure, fees and other terms are independently decided by the respective lender.
